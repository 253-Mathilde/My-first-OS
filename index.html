-----------frequently asked question-----------

Why does the timer not work?
↪ It works but it only counts minutes so it may look like it does not work;but trust me, it does! The timer is pretty simple for a reason and it is great for like the Pomodoro method or just helping you to stay focused.
How do I change the location for the weather widget?
↪ You don't need to because it gets your location automatically.I am sorry for all the people who use F instead of C,but I am still a beginner and you can google it: C is far more famous then F. How do I see that the time is finished?>
↪ When the time is counting down the button is grey, and when it's finished the color is normal and you can start a new timer.
The preloader does not work/the sound and the animations are not synced/the animation stops at the beginning.
↪ The animation should work properly if that's not the case you may refreshed the page incorrectly.Try closing the site and opening it again. If that problem appears again and nothing else helps, you unfortunately need to press Ctrl and F5.
